## Les deux fresques (Nano Banana)
**Contrainte commune — même composition**, pour que le sable passe d'une image à l'autre sans saut : format 16:9 ; ligne d'horizon à
60 % de la hauteur ; le sujet principal dans le **tiers gauche**, une file de personnages qui avance **vers la droite** (vers
l'est : le sable part dans ce sens) ; le **tiers droit plus calme** (c'est là qu'apparaîtra le signe 𠂤) ; aucun texte, aucune
signature, aucune bordure.

### Fresque A — `fresque_egypte.jpg`
> Egyptian New Kingdom wall painting in the Ramesside style, painted on smooth plaster: flat mineral colours (yellow ochre,
> red ochre, Egyptian blue, malachite green, carbon black outlines), figures in strict profile with the canonical Egyptian
> proportions, registers separated by thin painted bands. Subject: the carving of the great rock temple of Abu Simbel in
> Nubia. On the left third, the sandstone cliff face with two colossal seated statues of the king, their faces still being
> finished; tiny sculptors stand on wooden scaffolds and ladders with copper chisels and wooden mallets. A file of workers
> walks to the right along the lower register, carrying baskets of stone chips and water jars toward the Nile, which runs
> as a band of blue zig-zag water lines along the bottom. Pale ochre sky area in the right third, nearly empty, with a few
> small swallows. Horizon line at 60 % of the image height. Aged plaster texture with fine cracks and slightly faded
> pigments, soft warm light. 16:9, high detail, no text, no hieroglyph inscriptions, no border, no signature.

### Fresque B — `fresque_shang.jpg`
> Modern Chinese ink-wash painting (shuimo) on xuan paper, light mineral colour washes (pale ochre, faded malachite, a
> touch of vermilion), visible paper fibres, generous empty paper. Subject: Anyang on the Huan River, around 1250 BC, at
> dawn. On the left third, a raised rammed-earth platform with a thatched-roof hall on timber posts (no tiles, no curved
> eaves, no pagoda); in front of it a diviner in a knee-length cross-collar robe kneels over a small fire, holding a
> cleaned ox shoulder blade with a bronze rod, a thin thread of smoke rising. A file of soldiers walks to the right along
> the riverbank, carrying bronze dagger-axes (ge) on long wooden shafts and small rectangular shields, one of them holding
> a narrow vermilion banner. The Huan River crosses the bottom as soft grey wash. Distant low hills in pale ink, mist; the
> right third is almost empty paper with mist. Horizon line at 60 % of the image height. 16:9, high detail, no text, no
> calligraphy, no seal, no border, no signature.

> Note historique : la fresque B est une **peinture d'aujourd'hui** (il n'existe pas de fresque Shang connue) ; légende
> discrète à l'écran : « Anyang, vers 1250 av. J.-C. — vue d'artiste ». Pas de tuiles ni de toits relevés (anachroniques).
