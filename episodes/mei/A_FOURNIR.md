## 2. Clip A « le danseur » (bloc 3, et ombre de l'intro) → `images/danseur_depart.png`, `clips/03_danseur.mp4`
La pose finale doit reprendre le signe de l'os : **homme de face, bras ouverts, jambes écartées, quatre longues plumes
dressées sur la tête** — le clip entre à travers la forme `00` et sert aussi d'ombre derrière le papier dans l'intro.

### Image de départ (Nano Banana, 16:9)
```
3D animated film style, cinematic, soft global illumination. A young male ritual dancer stands facing the camera,
full body, in the center of a rammed-earth platform at dusk. His pose is perfectly frontal and symmetrical: arms
spread wide and slightly raised, legs apart, bare feet firmly planted. On his head, a tall headdress made of four
long pheasant tail feathers rising straight up, banded brown and gold, fixed in a bronze-and-jade headband.
He wears a knee-length dark brown tunic with a geometric spiral border woven in red and black, a wide leather belt,
a small carved jade pendant on the chest, bare arms painted with faint ochre stripes.
Behind him, out of focus: a low wooden hall with a thatched roof on a pounded-earth terrace, two large bronze ritual
vessels with animal-mask decoration, a crackling wood fire on his left lighting him with warm orange light, a deep
blue evening sky. Low camera, slightly upward angle, the feathers clearly outlined against the sky.
Clean, readable silhouette, no text, no letters, no other people in the foreground.
```

### Prompt Seedance (10 s, à partir de l'image)
```
The ritual dancer begins a slow, solemn dance in place: he sways from one foot to the other, his open arms rise and
fall like wings, his head tilts and the four tall pheasant feathers sway and catch the firelight. Sparks rise from
the fire, the flames flicker on his painted arms. Slow push-in of the camera from a full shot to a medium-full shot,
low angle. In the last two seconds he stops and freezes in a perfectly frontal, symmetrical pose: arms spread wide,
legs apart, the four feathers standing straight up against the evening sky, like a living pictogram.
Style: 3D animated film, cinematic, warm firelight against cool blue dusk, shallow depth of field.
No text, no subtitles, no music, no voice; ambient sound only: crackling fire, distant wind.
```

## Danseur v2 — image de référence plus fidèle aux Shang (Nano Banana 2)
```
3D animated film, semi-realistic cartoon render, lived-in surfaces with subtle wear, warm cinematic lighting. Wide frontal shot, 16:9, figure centered.
Lean young rain dancer standing face-on, legs planted wide apart, arms spread down and outward like an open X, a long white oxtail tassel in each hand; tall headdress of four long pheasant tail feathers rising from a bronze headband with a small jade disc at the brow, the two outer feathers curving outward.
Knee-length hemp tunic with crossed right-over-left collar and narrow sleeves, borders woven with square thunder spirals, wide sash with a hanging knee-apron panel, bare feet on dry cracked earth.
Behind him a raised platform of rammed earth with a thatched hall on wooden posts; a drummer at a leather drum on a wooden frame, three bronze bells mouth-up on a stand, a bronze tripod cauldron with thin incense smoke; villagers in hemp tunics watching.
Heavy grey storm clouds gathering over a parched millet field, low warm amber sun from the side, cool blue shadow under the clouds, dust lifting at his feet.
Solemn, hopeful mood. Soft depth of field, matte render, warm color grading, layered composition with foreground detail, no excessive ornamentation.
```
Notes : tunique courte à col croisé, ceinture et tablier de genoux (statuettes de jade de la tombe de Fu Hao, Anyang) ; motifs
de « tonnerre » 雷紋 ; queues de bœuf tenues à la main (le glyphe 舞 montre un danseur avec des pompons) ; cloches 鐃 posées
bouche en haut ; terre damée (hangtu) et toits de chaume. Pas de fer, pas de tuiles, pas de toits courbés, pas de soie brodée d'or.
