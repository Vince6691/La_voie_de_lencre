# 美 — à fournir (images et clips)

## 1. Image de référence — portrait-calligramme de Laozi → `images/laozi_reference.png`
Usage : contour suivi par les lattes de bambou, lavis de remplissage, détails au pinceau fin (bloc 5).
Format 9:16 ou 2:3, fond uni. Générer 3–4 variantes, garder la silhouette la plus lisible.

```
Traditional Chinese ink wash painting (shuimo), full-body portrait of a very old sage seen in strict side profile,
facing right, standing still on plain off-white xuan rice paper. Long flowing white beard reaching his chest, long
white eyebrows, bald crown with the remaining white hair gathered in a small topknot tied with a plain cloth band.
He wears a long, loose hemp robe with very wide sleeves, the collar crossed right over left, a simple cloth sash at
the waist, the hem falling to the ground; his hands rest together on top of a tall, slightly crooked wooden walking
staff. Calm, slightly bowed posture, serene expression, eyes half closed.
Style: expressive brush strokes, dark ink for the outline of the robe and staff, soft grey washes inside the robe
folds, fine brush lines for the face and beard, generous empty space around the figure. One single figure, centered,
clean and readable silhouette, nothing touching the edges.
Plain uniform paper background, no landscape, no ground line, no shadow, no text, no seal, no calligraphy, no frame,
no color, no gradient.
```
