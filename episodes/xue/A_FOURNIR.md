### Clip 1 — les devins (bloc 3, 12 s)
Entrée par la mer de nuages, sortie au pinceau au montage. Fenêtre à l'écran ≈ 11 s (de « Les devins » à « C'est là que notre signe apparaît »).

```
[Style global]: 3D cinematic cartoon, Pixar-quality rendering, warm amber firelight in a dim stone-and-timber ritual
hall, deep browns and bronze greens, thin smoke. Maintain consistent 3D cartoon style across all cuts, no
photorealistic drift. Duration: 12s, 16:9.

[00:00-00:06] Shot 1: The Heat (The Setup)
A diviner in a plain dark cross-collar robe and a tied-up hair knot kneels beside a low fire pit and lifts a long
pointed rod glowing red-hot from the embers. On a small stone slab in front of him lies a large tortoise plastron,
its inner surface carved with neat rows of small round hollows.
Camera: medium shot, slow dolly-in toward the glowing rod tip, eye level, normal lens.

[00:06-00:12] Shot 2: The Crack (The Payoff)
Close-up: the glowing tip presses into one hollow, a thin wisp of smoke rises, and a fine crack runs across the shell
with a small spark, branching into a T shape. A second diviner, older, leans in from the side to read the crack; his
eyes widen, his brush hand hovers above a strip of bamboo.
Camera: macro on the shell, then slow tilt up to the old diviner's face for the last 2 seconds.
Audio: soft fire crackle, a sharp dry crack.
Director's note: hold the crack's branching 1.5 s; the diviner reads it in silence.

Constraints: no text overlays, no readable characters, no extra main characters, no deformed hands, no snap zooms.
```
